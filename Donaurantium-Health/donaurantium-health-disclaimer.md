# Donaurantium Health — Disclaimer & Safety Notes

## What This Is

The protocols in this directory are **acoustic neural entrainment tools** — they use binaural beats and physical oscillation (bone conduction via headphone drivers) to gently encourage the brain toward specific oscillatory states. This is sometimes called "brainwave entrainment" or "frequency-following response" (FFR) entrainment.

This is **brain state modulation through sound**, not:
- Gene therapy
- Epigenetic editing
- Drug administration
- Medical treatment of any kind

The mechanisms are:
1. **Binaural beats** → frequency-following response → shifts in neural oscillation patterns
2. **Physical tones** → headphone diaphragm vibration → bone conduction → mechanosensory input → somatosensory cortex

These are real, measurable physiological effects studied in the peer-reviewed literature. They are also **mild** and **transient** — the state lasts while the session is running and for a short period afterward. This is not a cure, not a replacement for treatment, and not a pharmaceutical-grade intervention.

## What the Science Says

Binaural beat entrainment has documented effects in research settings:
- Theta (4–8 Hz) entrainment correlates with relaxation, reduced anxiety, and meditative states
- Gamma (40 Hz) stimulation is associated with increased alertness and is being studied in Alzheimer's contexts
- The Adey biological window (6–20 Hz ELF) — the range at which endogenous calcium efflux from neural tissue is documented — forms the basis for the photonic protocol's 16 Hz layer
- Bone conduction at low frequencies produces measurable somatosensory responses

These protocols are designed with these mechanisms in mind. They are not randomly assembled; each frequency layer targets a documented neural pathway. The target effects are functional state shifts, not structural changes.

## Who Should NOT Use These

Do **not** use these protocols if you:

- Have **epilepsy or a seizure disorder** — rhythmic auditory and visual stimulation can trigger seizures in photosensitive individuals
- Have a **diagnosed psychiatric condition** being managed with medication — altered states interact unpredictably with antipsychotics, mood stabilizers, and some antidepressants
- Are **pregnant**
- Are **operating heavy machinery or driving** — these sessions are designed to induce relaxed or altered states
- Are a **minor** (under 18)
- Have **tinnitus or hyperacusis** that might be worsened by sustained tone exposure

If you are unsure, consult a physician before use.

## How to Use Safely

- **Always use stereo headphones** — binaural beats only work with separate ear channels; do not use mono output or speakers
- **Start at low volume** — 40–50% system volume is a good starting point; the physical layers should be barely perceptible as vibration, not painfully loud
- **Do not use while driving, operating machinery, or in any situation requiring alertness**
- **Sit or lie down** in a comfortable position before starting
- **Stop immediately** if you experience dizziness, nausea, visual discomfort, headache, or any unusual sensation that concerns you
- **Sessions are 30 or 60 minutes** — this is by design; do not loop sessions back-to-back for extended periods
- **Drink water** before and after — this is standard harm-reduction advice for any altered-state practice

## Expectations

These protocols produce **subtle to moderate effects** in most users. They are not comparable to pharmacological interventions. Expect:

- Mild relaxation
- Subtle mood shifts
- Increased body awareness
- Possible sensations of warmth or heaviness

Do not expect:
- Hallucinations
- Dissociation
- Effects equivalent to controlled substances

Some users report little to no effect. Individual response varies significantly based on baseline brain state, session conditions (lighting, comfort, focus), and general sensitivity to auditory stimulation.
