## How to Read This Document

This document estimates what a daily-session user might realistically experience at
each milestone, per protocol. Estimates are derived from the closest published evidence
to each implemented mechanism — not from the mechanism itself. Where no human data
exists, that gap is stated explicitly.

Three evidence tiers are used throughout:

```
[CONFIRMED]  — Direct human trial evidence at this timepoint
[EXTRAPOLATED] — Human evidence from similar mechanism (e.g., meditation standing in
                 for acoustic entrainment); effect plausible but not proven for acoustic
[SPECULATIVE] — Cell culture or animal data only; human in-vivo translation untested
```

**Responder warning upfront:** The research consistently shows 30–50% of people are
"non-responders" to entrainment and HPA-axis interventions. There is no a priori test
for responder status. The estimates below are for responders. If you see nothing after
60 days of daily sessions, you may not be in the responding population — that is a
valid scientific result, not a failure.

## Mechanism Confidence Map

Before the timelines, here is how well each implemented mechanism is supported for
headphone-level acoustic delivery in humans:

```
Mechanism                          Confidence    Strongest evidence
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Binaural beat FFR entrainment      MODERATE      Direct EEG studies, mixed results
Isochronic tone CAEP entrainment   MODERATE      Amplitude modulation following response
6 Hz theta → HPA/cortisol         EXTRAPOLATED  Meditation-HPA literature (Tang 2007)
10 Hz alpha → GABA/GABRA2         EXTRAPOLATED  Muthukumaraswamy GABA-alpha correlation
40 Hz GENUS → neuroinflammation   MODERATE      Tsai lab mice + early human pilot data
FKBP5 methylation shift           EXTRAPOLATED  9-week MBSR/PTSD study (PMC6153325)
SLC6A4 methylation                EXTRAPOLATED  Same MBSR study; very indirect
BDNF from 20 Hz                   EXTRAPOLATED  20 Hz EMS (not acoustic) → BDNF
PIEZO1/TRPV4 activation           SPECULATIVE   Cell culture; in-vivo coupling unproven
COL1A1 via 10/30/400/800 Hz       SPECULATIVE   BM-MSC cell culture only; 10 Hz untested
Pink noise stochastic resonance    SPECULATIVE   Proven for neuropathy; unclear in health
```

The most honest statement about acoustic mechanotransduction at headphone levels:
the physics suggests PIEZO1 threshold (15 pN) is theoretically achievable, but
tissue coupling in vivo is so poorly characterized that the expected biological
effect could range from negligible to modest. No human study has measured it.

## Universal Timeline: What Applies to All Protocols

These effects apply regardless of protocol variant. They are the most reliable
predictions.

### Session 1 — Day 1

**Acute state changes (during/immediately after session):**
- Mild cortical EEG shift toward entrained frequency band — measurable in lab, unlikely
  to be subjectively distinct from relaxed listening [CONFIRMED]
- Temporary parasympathetic activation — reduced heart rate, HRV increase (common
  in quiet listening sessions of any kind; not specific to entrainment) [CONFIRMED]
- Subjective relaxation — partly expectancy, partly real; cannot be separated without
  sham control [CONFIRMED, but confounded]

**What hasn't happened yet:** Nothing biological at the gene expression or protein level.
Epigenetic changes, BDNF elevation, FKBP5 methylation, collagen synthesis — none of
these change meaningfully from a single session.

### Days 1–7

**Habituation risk is already present.** Repeated identical stimulation causes neural
habituation: the brain progressively filters a constant signal as "unimportant." The
biological drift LFO (±3%, 55–90s period) in each physical layer is specifically
designed to slow this — but does not eliminate it.

**What to expect:**
- Deepening ability to enter a relaxed state during sessions (skill acquisition, not
  purely biological change) [CONFIRMED from meditation training literature]
- No measurable biomarker changes expected [CONFIRMED absence from all studies]
- Possible improved sleep onset for some users (theta → drowsiness crossover) [EXTRAPOLATED]

**The honest 7-day check:** The most important thing at 7 days is whether you can
complete sessions consistently. Compliance is the rate-limiting factor for everything
downstream.

## Anxiety Protocol

**Target genes:** FKBP5▼ SLC6A4 HTR2A▼ GABRA2▲ BDNF▲  
**Protocol emphasis:** 120s logarithmic fade-in (mandatory), extended theta, minimal
gamma (subthreshold maintenance only)  
**Key mechanistic bet:** Sustained 6 Hz theta → FKBP5 methylation accumulation →
HPA axis downregulation → cortisol baseline reduction

### 7 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Subjective anxiety | Mild reduction during/after sessions; modest carryover | EXTRAPOLATED |
| HRV | Acute increase during session; may not persist yet | CONFIRMED |
| Cortisol | No measurable change | CONFIRMED |
| FKBP5 methylation | No change | CONFIRMED |
| GABA | No change | SPECULATIVE |
| BDNF | No change | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Sleep | Possible modest improvement in sleep onset latency | EXTRAPOLATED |

**Realistic summary:** The sessions feel good. That's meaningful but not yet
biological. The 120-second fade-in doing its job means no startle activation of
the FKBP5/HPA axis you're trying to suppress.

### 30 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Subjective anxiety | Clinically noticeable reduction in 40–60% of consistent users | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Morning cortisol (awakening response) | Possible 10–20% reduction in responders | EXTRAPOLATED |
| HRV baseline | Modest improvement (3–8 ms RMSSD); daily tracking needed | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Cortisol stress reactivity | May begin to blunt; faster recovery from stressors | EXTRAPOLATED |
| EEG (if measured) | Frontal alpha power may increase; theta coherence during sessions | CONFIRMED |
| P300 amplitude | Enhancement possible after 4 weeks daily | CONFIRMED |
| FKBP5 methylation | Still unlikely; MBSR evidence points to 9 weeks | EXTRAPOLATED |
| BDNF | Possibly beginning to trend up; not stable yet | EXTRAPOLATED |
| GABA | Unknown; minimal change expected | SPECULATIVE |
| Habituation concern | Growing — sessions may feel less "effective" | CONFIRMED |

**Realistic summary:** This is where the first real biological signal might emerge.
Morning cortisol is the earliest reliable measurable proxy. Subjectively, users
who are responders often describe feeling "less wired" as background tension, not
a dramatic effect. Non-responders see nothing.

**Habituation warning at day 30:** The brain habituates to repeated identical stimuli.
Consider alternating protocol lengths (30 min vs. 60 min) or using the intensity
slider to slightly vary sessions.

### 60 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Subjective anxiety (GAD-7) | Possible 3–5 point reduction (roughly 1 clinical category) | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Morning cortisol | 15–25% reduction in responders | EXTRAPOLATED |
| HRV | Measurable baseline improvement in responders | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Cortisol stress reactivity | More consistent blunting; HPA tone visibly lower | EXTRAPOLATED |
| FKBP5 methylation | Possible early shift beginning; not yet stable | EXTRAPOLATED |
| SLC6A4 methylation | Unclear; may begin to shift with sustained stress reduction | EXTRAPOLATED |
| BDNF serum | Possible modest elevation (measuring this is expensive and variable) | EXTRAPOLATED |
| GABA | Still unknown; possibly marginal | SPECULATIVE |
| PIEZO1/mechanotransduction | Still negligible at headphone delivery levels | SPECULATIVE |

**Realistic summary:** If you're a responder, 60 days of daily 60-minute sessions
is where you might feel genuinely different. The mechanism is most likely: theta
entrainment → relaxation response → cortisol regulation → downstream gene expression
shifts. The FKBP5/BDNF/SLC6A4 pathway is real but slower.

### 90 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Subjective anxiety | Stable reduction; less acute reactivity | EXTRAPOLATED |
| FKBP5 methylation | Possible measurable shift in CpG_35558513 in blood (MBSR responders showed this at ~9 weeks) | EXTRAPOLATED |
| SLC6A4 methylation | Possible; same timeline | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Cortisol | Stable lower baseline in responders | EXTRAPOLATED |
| BDNF | Elevated in responders; may plateau | EXTRAPOLATED |
| GABA/GABRA2 | Still mostly unknown; indirect evidence at best | SPECULATIVE |
| HTR2A expression | Unknown; no acoustic trial data | SPECULATIVE |

**Realistic summary:** 90 days is the first point where a blood methylation test
(if ordered) might show something. The MBSR literature shows 9-week changes in
FKBP5 intron 7 methylation in responders. Acoustic theta is not MBSR but shares
theta entrainment and stress-reduction mechanisms.

**Beyond 90 days:** Unknown territory. Possible continued deepening of epigenetic
effects. Habituation accumulation becomes a real threat — sustained epigenetic
change may require continued but varied practice, not identical daily repetition.
Consider cycling: 12 weeks on / 2 weeks rest.

## Spinal Stenosis Protocol

**Target genes:** FKBP5▼ OPRM1 FAAH BDNF (no spinal dorsal horn BDNF) COL1A1▲  
**Key exclusion:** BDNF_20HZ is excluded — spinal dorsal horn BDNF is pro-nociceptive  
**Key mechanistic bet:** COL1A1 synthesis via 10/30/400/800 Hz acoustic → disc
preservation and collagen drive; pain gate via sub-theta + alpha entrainment  
**Honest difficulty level:** This protocol has the largest gap between mechanism
evidence and human in-vivo delivery

### 7 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Pain (NRS/VAS) | Possible modest acute reduction during/after sessions | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Muscle tension | Reduction in paravertebral tension possible (relaxation response) | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Inflammation markers | No change | — |
| COL1A1 / collagen | No change | SPECULATIVE |
| Disc structure | No change | SPECULATIVE |

**Realistic summary:** Week 1 for spinal stenosis is primarily about pain gate
(sub-threshold pain signal modulation via alpha/theta) and relaxation of protective
muscle guarding. These are real and fast. They are not disc repair.

### 30 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Pain (NRS daily average) | Possible 1–2 point reduction in responders | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Mobility / ROM | Possible modest improvement if guarding is reducing | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Cortisol (stress-pain coupling) | Beginning to shift; stress amplifies pain — reducing cortisol helps | EXTRAPOLATED |
| FAAH / endocannabinoid tone | Unknown; no acoustic data; speculative | SPECULATIVE |
| Serum collagen markers (P1NP) | Possibly beginning to trend; no human data | SPECULATIVE |
| Disc hydration (MRI T2) | No change at 30 days | SPECULATIVE |
| OPRM1 / opioid receptor | Unknown | SPECULATIVE |

**Realistic summary:** Pain reduction at day 30 is most likely mediated by the
anxiety/HPA axis arm (cortisol reduction, parasympathetic shift reducing
neuroinflammatory load) rather than structural repair. Structural repair is months-
to-years biology.

### 60 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | Possible stable 1–3 point reduction in responders | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Physical function | Possible modest improvement in daily activity tolerance | EXTRAPOLATED |

| Disc structure (imaging) | No measurable change; too early | SPECULATIVE |
| COL1A1 cell culture equivalent | Cell culture shows COL1A1 mRNA increase at day 7–14; in-vivo lag unknown | SPECULATIVE |

**Realistic summary:** At 60 days, the most honest framing is: the protocol may
be providing meaningful pain modulation through neural and hormonal pathways, while
the structural (collagen/disc) hypothesis is still unproven for headphone delivery.

### 90 Days

| What | Realistic Expectation | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | Stable reduction; reduced need for pain-reactive guarding | EXTRAPOLATED |
| Collagen/structural markers | First point where in-vivo collagen effects might be detectable (serum markers) | SPECULATIVE |
| Disc hydration (MRI T2) | Possibly beginning to trend in responders; no human data | SPECULATIVE |
| Mobility | Improved; partly pain reduction, partly muscle function | EXTRAPOLATED |
| OPRM1/endocannabinoid | Unknown; possibly improved endogenous opioid tone via theta | SPECULATIVE |

**Beyond 90 days:** Connective tissue biology is slow. Collagen turnover in disc
tissue operates on a timescale of months to years. If the acoustic COL1A1 hypothesis
is correct, the 6-month mark is where structural evidence would first plausibly
appear on imaging. This is outside any published human data for acoustic collagen.

**Important structural note:** For spinal stenosis, this protocol is most honestly
understood as **pain modulation + anti-inflammatory support + biological scaffolding
for repair** — not as a direct structural repair intervention at headphone delivery
levels. If structural change occurs, it is a secondary consequence of reduced
inflammatory load and possibly improved cellular signaling, not direct acoustic
machining of disc tissue.

## Cross-Protocol: Habituation Management

**The most important thing the science says about sustained use:**

Neural habituation is documented to begin as early as day 19 with repeated identical
entrainment stimulation. Efficacy per session declines. The biological drift LFO
(±3%, ~55–90s period) and harmonic reinforcement in the code delay this but do not
prevent it.

**Recommended habituation management:**

| Timepoint | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Consistent daily sessions; build the habit; use intensity slider at 1.0× |
| Days 30–60 | Begin alternating 30 and 60-minute sessions; vary intensity slider ±0.2× |
| Days 60–90 | Consider biweekly "rest days"; optionally alternate protocols |
| Day 90+ | Consider 12-week active / 2-week rest cycles |

## What Would Constitute a Meaningful Result

For each protocol, the minimum bar to call the experiment "positive" at 90 days:

**Anxiety protocol:**
- Sustained 3+ point GAD-7 reduction, AND
- Morning cortisol reduced ≥10% from baseline

**Depression protocol:**
- Sustained 5+ point PHQ-9 reduction, AND
- BDNF serum elevation ≥20% from baseline

**Spinal stenosis protocol:**
- Sustained 2+ point NRS pain reduction, AND
- Measurable improvement in physical function score (PROMIS-Physical Function)

**General improvement protocol:**
- RMSSD HRV improved ≥5 ms from baseline, AND
- Sustained improvement in any primary subjective outcome scale

## Ceiling Predictions: What Probably Cannot Happen

Even at 6–12 months of perfect compliance:

- **Direct disc structural repair** from headphone acoustic pressure — unlikely to be
  attributable to acoustic alone; no human mechanism established
- **PIEZO1-driven gene expression changes measurable in blood** — possible in theory;
  no in-vivo human evidence; don't design experiments expecting this signal
- **Reversal of established anxiety/depression without behavioral/social changes** —
  the biology can be shifted toward better plasticity; what you do in that window
  matters as much as the protocol
- **40 Hz amyloid clearance** (for general improvement) — human translation from mouse
  models is preliminary; this is an adjunct, not a primary intervention for cognitive
  decline

## The Most Likely Actual Mechanism of Benefit

If benefits occur, the most parsimonious explanation compatible with existing evidence:

```
Daily acoustic sessions
    → Reliable daily cortical quieting (auditory rest-state engagement)
    → Parasympathetic activation / HRV improvement
    → Reduced HPA axis reactivity (cortisol baseline drops)
    → Downstream: better sleep, reduced neuroinflammatory load,
      improved prefrontal regulation, easier emotional processing
    → Over weeks: epigenetic shifts (FKBP5, SLC6A4) in responders
    → Over months: BDNF elevation, neuroplasticity scaffolding

The frequency-specific gene targeting (specific Hz → specific gene) adds
mechanistic precision to what is partly a sophisticated structured relaxation
intervention. The precision may matter at the margins; the broader stress-
reduction cascade matters everywhere.
```

This is not a dismissal of the mechanism. It is an honest integration: the
protocol is doing both things simultaneously, and we cannot cleanly separate them.
The gene-targeting design is the scientifically motivated upper bound on what
structured acoustic relaxation might accomplish.

## Longitudinal Adaptation Beyond 90 Days

If the 90-day results are positive, the question becomes sustainability.

**What the meditation literature says (closest long-term analogue):**
- 4+ years of sustained practice shows measurable gray matter increases (hippocampus,
  prefrontal cortex, insula) [CONFIRMED in long-term meditators]
- Cortisol response flattening becomes stable and durable [CONFIRMED]
- Telomere length may increase with sustained practice [EXTRAPOLATED; Blackburn et al.]
- The mechanisms stabilize — the epigenetics are increasingly baked in, requiring
  less active input to maintain

**Prediction for Donaurantium beyond 90 days:**
- Year 1: Consolidation. The epigenetic shifts stabilize. Habituation requires
  increasing protocol variation.
- Year 2: If COL1A1/structural changes are occurring (spinal), this is the earliest
  window where imaging might show them.
- 4+ years: Unknown. The acoustic epigenetics field has no longitudinal human data.
  We are extrapolating from meditation literature with mechanistic justification.
