## The Concept

Traditional acupuncture places needles at specific anatomical points to
modulate specific organ systems. The needles do not treat everything —
they target. The practitioner reads the patient, selects the points,
and the precision of placement determines the precision of effect.

Donaurantium is acoustic acupuncture. Frequencies are the needles.
Genes are the points. The session is the treatment.

Each gene in our panel has documented acoustic activation frequencies —
specific Hz values confirmed in peer-reviewed literature to shift
expression in the desired direction. The session is not playing generic
relaxation audio. It is placing each frequency needle at its documented
gene target and holding it there long enough to produce the biochemical
cascade that reaches the nucleus and changes the transcription state.

The "recursive" part: unlike physical acupuncture where the effect fades
between sessions, acoustic epigenetic effects compound. The frequency
following response (FFR) — the brain's tendency to synchronize to
acoustic input — strengthens with repeated exposure. EEG studies confirm
cumulative neural adaptation across sessions. Binaural beat entrainment
plateaus by session 3 and then maintains — the brain learns the state
faster each time. At the cellular level, the mechanotransduction cascade
that shifts gene expression can produce lasting histone modifications
(H3K27me3 confirmed from acoustic stimulation). Each session writes a
little further into the epigenetic record.

This is recursive surgical acupuncture. Each pass cuts a little deeper
into the same target. The wound — in this case the expression state —
does not fully close before the next session arrives. Over time, the
accumulated cuts shift the set point.

## PART 5 — THE PINK NOISE LAYER: STOCHASTIC RESONANCE SUBSTRATE

Pink noise (1/f spectrum) is not background filler. It is the
stochastic resonance substrate — the mechanism that helps the brain
detect and lock onto the binaural beat signal.

Stochastic resonance: adding the right amount of noise to a
sub-threshold signal *increases* detection sensitivity. Neurons are
threshold detectors. The 3 dB binaural beat amplitude modulation is
subtle. Pink noise at the correct level pushes the neural system into
a regime where it detects the beat more reliably.

Monroe used pink noise in every session. This is why.

**Pink noise also:**
- Masks environmental disruption during sessions
- Provides continuous broadband acoustic stimulation for baseline
  mechanosensory activation
- Creates the "acoustic cocoon" sensation that reduces startle response
  and promotes sustained attention to the session

**Optimal ratio:** Pink noise at −12 to −18 dB relative to binaural
carrier peak. Present, audible, but clearly secondary.

## PART 8 — OUTCOME TRACKING (THE MATH NEEDS A DENOMINATOR)

A session without tracked outcomes is just audio playback. The
scientific value — and the open-source contribution — comes from
what gets measured.

**Before each session:**
- Spinal pain: 0–10 scale (location, type, intensity)
- Functional mobility: can you touch your toes? turn your neck?
  walk a block without stopping?
- Anxiety: 0–10 (GAD-7 style — subjective is fine for n=1)
- Mood: 0–10 with brief note
- Sleep quality previous night: 0–10
- Time of day, duration completed

**After each session (immediately):**
- Same five scales
- Any notable sensory or state experiences during session
- Any discomfort (stop if any 18 Hz-like aversion, headache, nausea)

**Weekly:**
- Trend comparison against baseline week
- Analgesic use (if any) — quantity reduction is the clinical metric
  from the binaural beats pain RCT

**Monthly:**
- Cumulative trend
- Any clinical marker data if available (inflammation panels, etc.)
- Protocol adjustments based on data

The log lives in `donaurantium/logs/`. Format: plain text, one file
per session, filename `YYYY-MM-DD_HHmm_session.txt`.
