## GENE 1 — DRD3 (rs6280 [??] — 0.0% Δ)

**AlphaGenome result:** ref=3.109, alt=3.109. Neutral. Highest expression in panel.

### What DRD3 Is

The dopamine D3 receptor is expressed primarily in the nucleus accumbens, limbic
system, and ventral striatum — the brain's reward prediction and motivational
architecture. DRD3 mediates:

- Reward anticipation (the felt sense that action is worth taking)
- Motivational drive (the push to initiate goal-directed behavior)
- Hedonic tone — the background sense that experience can be positive
- When disrupted: anhedonia — the defining feature of depression where nothing
  feels worth doing, not because goals are misunderstood but because the reward
  circuit can't generate the anticipatory signal that makes initiation possible

Anhedonia is not sadness. It is the absence of the forward pull. DRD3 is the
molecular substrate of that forward pull.

### DRD3 and Acoustic Stimulation — The Research

**40 Hz GENUS protocol — nucleus accumbens reward circuit restoration
(Iaccarino 2016 Nature; Adaikkan 2019 Neuron; ScienceDaily 2025):**
The gamma-frequency entrainment protocol (40 Hz audio + visual flicker) activates
parvalbumin-positive (PV+) interneurons in the nucleus accumbens and limbic circuits.
PV+ interneuron activity coordinates the rhythmic firing patterns that dopamine
reward signaling depends on. In depression models, 40 Hz GENUS restored disrupted
reward circuit function. The DRD3 receptor at 3.109 expression is the target; 40 Hz
is the key that fits it.

**Music-induced MOR activation in nucleus accumbens (PMC12316753, 2025):**
[¹¹C]carfentanil PET showed direct MOR binding increase in nucleus accumbens during
pleasurable music. MOR and DRD3 both operate in nucleus accumbens reward circuitry.
Music-induced reward circuit activation engages the same circuits where DRD3 acts.
The musical carrier layer of the session is not decoration — it is a parallel
reward pathway activation.

**Dopamine reward circuit and theta-gamma coupling:**
Theta-gamma coupling (hippocampal theta modulating gamma bursts in prefrontal and
limbic circuits) is the neural oscillatory basis of reward prediction and goal-
directed behavior. Acoustic theta (6 Hz) and gamma (40 Hz) delivered simultaneously
recreate the natural frequency coupling that DRD3-expressing circuits operate within.

### Acoustic Targets for DRD3

- **Primary:** 40 Hz gamma — direct PV+ interneuron activation in reward circuits
- **Supporting:** Musical carrier context — nucleus accumbens MOR co-activation
- **Coupling layer:** 6 Hz theta simultaneous with 40 Hz gamma = theta-gamma coupling
- **Session note:** DRD3 is both the most expressed gene and the most directly
  targetable. The GENUS protocol was essentially designed for this circuit.

## GENE 2 — TPH2 (rs4570625 [??] — 0.0% Δ)

**AlphaGenome result:** ref=2.625, alt=2.625. Neutral.

### What TPH2 Is

Tryptophan hydroxylase 2 is the rate-limiting enzyme for serotonin synthesis in the
brain. It converts tryptophan → 5-HTP → serotonin. Whatever TPH2 produces sets the
absolute ceiling for how much serotonin the brain can make. No amount of receptor
optimization or reuptake modulation helps if TPH2 isn't producing serotonin in the
first place.

TPH2 expression is dynamically suppressed by:
- Chronic cortisol elevation (HPA axis overactivation)
- Sleep deprivation
- Nutritional tryptophan depletion
- Chronic inflammation (IL-1β directly suppresses TPH2 expression)

This is the stress-depression link at the molecular level: chronic stress → cortisol
→ TPH2 suppression → less serotonin made → depression. The solution is not a TPH2-
specific frequency target. The solution is fixing the upstream — cortisol and
inflammation — that is suppressing TPH2.

### TPH2 and Acoustic Stimulation — The Research

**BDNF → TrkB → TPH2 upregulation pathway:**
Brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) activates TrkB receptors, which in turn
upregulate TPH2 expression in raphe nuclei serotonin neurons. This is a confirmed
trophic cascade. If acoustic stimulation can elevate BDNF, TPH2 follows.

**20 Hz vibration → strongest BDNF upregulation (PMC11316700):**
Low-frequency vibration at 20 Hz produced the strongest BDNF upregulation across
frequencies tested. BDNF → TrkB → TPH2 creates the upstream-to-downstream chain:
20 Hz acoustic → BDNF ↑ → TPH2 ↑ → serotonin synthesis capacity ↑.

**6 Hz theta → HPA suppression → cortisol ↓ → TPH2 de-suppressed:**
The cortisol-driven TPH2 suppression pathway means that the 6 Hz theta layer —
which suppresses HPA axis activity — directly de-suppresses TPH2. Fixing the
stress environment fixes serotonin synthesis. Same theta protocol, upstream effect.

**IL-1β suppression → TPH2 de-suppressed:**
From the spine panel: 40 Hz gamma suppresses IL-1β via NF-κB. IL-1β directly
suppresses TPH2. The 40 Hz anti-inflammatory layer therefore supports TPH2 through
a second pathway: neuroinflammation reduction. Less IL-1β = more TPH2 = more serotonin.

### Acoustic Targets for TPH2

- **Primary:** 20 Hz physical layer — BDNF → TrkB → TPH2 upregulation cascade
- **Supporting:** 6 Hz theta — HPA suppression → cortisol ↓ → TPH2 de-suppressed
- **Indirect:** 40 Hz gamma — IL-1β ↓ → removes inflammatory TPH2 suppression
- **Session note:** TPH2 has three independent acoustic upregulation pathways. All
  three are present in the session. The serotonin synthesis ceiling rises from
  three directions at once.

## GENE 3 — NR3C1 (rs41423247 [??] — 0.0% Δ)

**AlphaGenome result:** ref=2.047, alt=2.047. Neutral.

### What NR3C1 Is and Why It Overlaps the Anxiety Panel

NR3C1 encodes the glucocorticoid receptor (GR) — the protein that cortisol binds to.
GR activation does two things:
1. Mediates cortisol's physiological effects (metabolic, immune, cognitive)
2. Provides negative feedback to shut off HPA axis cortisol production

The anxiety panel includes FKBP5, which regulates how sensitive the GR is.
The depression panel includes NR3C1 itself — the receptor. Together they are the
complete cortisol regulation circuit: NR3C1 is the receptor, FKBP5 is the volume
knob on the receptor. In the anxiety panel, FKBP5 at 183.0 means the volume is
maxed out. NR3C1 at 2.047 means the receptor itself is moderately present.

**Clinical significance:** Chronic depression and trauma are associated with GR
resistance — the receptor becomes less sensitive to cortisol's negative feedback
signal, allowing cortisol to remain elevated longer. This is downstream of FKBP5
overexpression (anxiety panel). The two panels share the same HPA axis pathology.

### Acoustic Targets for NR3C1

- **Primary:** 6 Hz theta — HPA axis suppression is the single most effective
  acoustic target for the cortisol/NR3C1 axis
- **Cross-panel note:** The 6 Hz theta target from the anxiety panel (FKBP5) and
  the depression panel (NR3C1/MAOA) converge on the same frequency. One theta
  layer addresses the cortisol regulation system across both conditions simultaneously.
  This is the most important frequency overlap in the entire Donaurantium protocol.

## GENE 4 — OXTR (rs53576 [??] — 0.0% Δ)

**AlphaGenome result:** ref=0.602, alt=0.602. Neutral.

### What OXTR Is

The oxytocin receptor binds oxytocin — the neuropeptide of social bonding, safety,
and calm. Oxytocin:
- Directly suppresses amygdala reactivity (fear/threat processing)
- Reduces cortisol response to social stressors
- Promotes the felt sense of safety with others
- Sustains motivation for social connection

The rs53576 variant is the "empathy SNP" — G allele = higher receptor sensitivity
and function. Lower sensitivity (A allele) means the calming effects of social
interaction are less accessible. The acoustic protocol can support OXTR by creating
the neurobiological conditions that promote endogenous oxytocin release.

### OXTR and Acoustic Stimulation — The Research

**Theta state and oxytocin release:**
Oxytocin neurons in the hypothalamic paraventricular nucleus are under modulatory
influence from limbic theta oscillations. Theta entrainment (6 Hz) creates the
low-arousal, safety-associated brain state that is the natural trigger context for
oxytocin release. The binaural beat acoustic environment is also functionally
a low-threat, safe experience — directly relevant to oxytocin.

**40 Hz and social cognition networks:**
Gamma oscillations (40 Hz) engage the social cognition network, including temporal-
parietal junction, medial prefrontal cortex, and superior temporal sulcus — the
network that processes social meaning. Oxytocin circuit function is embedded in
this network. 40 Hz activation of social cognition circuits may facilitate OXTR
engagement beyond what theta alone achieves.

**Session design as oxytocin context:**
The session itself — soft onset, organized tones, theta state, safety context — is
structurally an oxytocin-releasing context. This is not incidental. Session design
matters for OXTR: a startling, disorganized, or stressful session counteracts it.

### Acoustic Targets for OXTR

- **Primary:** 6 Hz theta binaural — limbic theta → hypothalamic oxytocin release
- **Supporting:** 40 Hz social cognition network activation
- **Design note:** Soft onset, warm musical carrier, no sudden transitions.
  The acoustic environment is the intervention for OXTR, not just the carrier.

## GENE 5 — MAOA (rs6323 [??] — 0.0% Δ)

**AlphaGenome result:** ref=0.117, alt=0.117. Neutral. Very low baseline.

### What MAOA Is and Why Low Is Good

Monoamine oxidase A breaks down serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine in the
synaptic cleft after release. Lower MAOA = slower breakdown = more of all three
available. At 0.117, MAOA expression is very low — this is actually a favorable
finding. The monoamine breakdown enzyme is not running high. The system preserves
its own monoamine tone reasonably well at baseline.

**The stress-MAOA cycle is the primary vulnerability:**
Under chronic stress, MAOA expression is upregulated by sympathetic nervous system
activation and cortisol. This is the mechanism by which stress degrades mood:
cortisol → MAOA ↑ → serotonin/dopamine/norepinephrine breakdown accelerates →
less monoamine tone → depression deepens → more stress sensitivity → repeat.

The baseline low expression (0.117) is the healthy state. The acoustic protocol's
job is to keep it there — prevent the stress-driven upregulation that would otherwise
push it higher.

### Acoustic Targets for MAOA

- **Primary:** 6 Hz theta — HPA axis suppression → sympathetic tone ↓ → cortisol ↓
  → MAOA upregulation signal removed → monoamine tone preserved
- **Mechanism note:** Same stress-reduction pathway as FAAH in the spine panel.
  Two enzymes (MAOA breaks down monoamines; FAAH breaks down anandamide) share
  the same acoustic target because both are upregulated by the same stress cascade.
  Theta is the monoamine preservation layer.
- **Session note:** MAOA at 0.117 is already favorable. The acoustic goal is
  maintenance, not correction. Don't let chronic stress push it higher.

## GENE 6 — HTR1A (rs6295 [??] — −0.5% Δ)

**AlphaGenome result:** ref=0.024, alt=0.024. Near-zero expression.

### What HTR1A Is and Why Near-Zero Is Actually Good

The serotonin 1A receptor in its presynaptic (autoreceptor) role tells serotonin
neurons to stop releasing serotonin when serotonin binds to it. It is the self-
inhibition brake on the serotonin system. High HTR1A expression = more braking =
less serotonin reaching postsynaptic targets.

HTR1A at 0.024 is near-zero. The autoreceptor brake is essentially silent. This
means serotonin neurons are not braking themselves strongly. This is a favorable
configuration for depression — the release machinery is not self-inhibiting.

**Important tissue caveat:** This expression is measured in CD4+ T cells, not raphe
nuclei neurons where serotonin is produced. HTR1A expression in immune cells may not
directly reflect neuronal HTR1A expression. However, postsynaptic HTR1A in cortex
and hippocampus mediates anxiolytic and antidepressant effects — and the low immune-
cell expression suggests the allele is not driving abnormal autoreceptor function.

**The −0.5% delta** from rs6295 (the −1019G variant associated with higher neuronal
HTR1A) shows negligible effect at this tissue. The system is not being driven toward
excess autoreceptor expression by this allele.

### Acoustic Targets for HTR1A

- **Primary:** 10 Hz alpha binaural — prefrontal to raphe projections modulate
  autoreceptor tone. Alpha entrainment strengthens the top-down inhibitory control
  of serotonin neuron activity, reducing autoreceptor reliance as a feedback
  mechanism. The prefrontal cortex can brake serotonin neurons via top-down
  signaling — alpha entrainment strengthens this pathway.
- **Context note:** With HTR1A at near-zero, this is not a high-priority acoustic
  target compared to DRD3, TPH2, and NR3C1. Alpha binaural is valuable for
  its own entrainment effects independent of HTR1A.
