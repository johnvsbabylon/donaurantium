## GENE 1 — NOS3 (Endothelial Nitric Oxide Synthase)

### What NOS3 Is

NOS3 (eNOS) generates nitric oxide (NO) in the vascular endothelium. NO is
the primary signal for blood vessel relaxation. NOS3 activation → NO → smooth
muscle relaxation → vasodilation → lower blood pressure → improved blood flow
to every tissue in the body. NOS3 is also the most directly mechanosensitive
gene in the entire Donaurantium protocol — it is literally activated by
physical forces on the blood vessel wall.

Reduced NOS3 function = less NO = vessels stay contracted = chronic
hypertension = cardiovascular risk. The Glu298Asp variant (rs1799983, T allele)
is associated with reduced NOS3 activity and elevated cardiovascular risk.

### NOS3 and Acoustic Stimulation — The Most Direct Cardiovascular Pathway

**PIEZO1 is the acoustic gateway to NOS3 (PMC5127677):**

This is the most mechanistically direct acoustic → cardiovascular pathway
in the sonobiology literature. The full confirmed chain:

```
Shear stress / acoustic vibration
         ↓
PIEZO1 mechanosensitive channel opens (endothelial cells)
         ↓
Ca²⁺ influx
         ↓
ATP release via pannexin channels
         ↓
P2Y2/Gq/G11 receptor activation
         ↓
AKT phosphorylation
         ↓
eNOS (NOS3) phosphorylation at Ser1179 and Ser635
         ↓
Nitric oxide (NO) produced
         ↓
Vascular smooth muscle relaxation
         ↓
Vasodilation → blood pressure reduction
```

Mice with endothelium-specific PIEZO1 deletion developed hypertension of
**+10–15 mmHg within days** — the fastest, most direct demonstration that
the mechanosensory → NOS3 → NO pathway is active, non-redundant, and
acoustically accessible. PIEZO1 senses physical forces. Acoustic stimulation
delivers physical forces. The mechanism works.

**NOS3 and cellular regeneration (PMC11616527, PubMed 14556003):**
NOS3 activation doesn't only regulate blood pressure — it mobilizes:
- Endothelial progenitor cells (EPCs) from bone marrow → vascular repair
- Stem and progenitor cell recruitment (eNOS expressed by bone marrow
  stromal cells directly influences EPC mobilization)
- Tubulogenesis: NOS3 activation enhances vessel formation and
  angiogenesis. Acoustic NOS3 upregulation = new capillary growth.

This is the cardiovascular regeneration pathway. The same acoustic signal
that reduces blood pressure also calls endothelial progenitor cells to
repair damaged vessel walls. Sound as vascular regeneration.

### Acoustic Targets for NOS3

- **Supporting:** 6 Hz theta → vagal tone ↑ → parasympathetic → vessels relax

## GENE 2 — IL10 (Interleukin-10)

### What IL10 Is

IL-10 is the master anti-inflammatory cytokine — the biological counterpart
to IL-1β, IL-6, and TNF-α from the spine and general health panels.
Where those cytokines drive inflammation, IL-10 terminates it:

- Directly inhibits TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6, and IL-12 production
- Downregulates MHC-II expression → reduces antigen presentation → calms
  autoimmune activation
- Activates JAK/STAT3 signaling → suppresses cytokine gene expression broadly
- In somatosensory neurons, IL-10 controls CCL2 release → reduces
  inflammatory pain sensitization (PubMed 38081433)

Higher IL-10 = better immune self-regulation = less autoimmune activity =
less chronic inflammation = better recovery from every other condition in
this protocol. IL-10 is the immune system's off switch. Acoustically
upregulating it is as important as suppressing IL-1β and TNF-α.

- **Mechanism:** p38 MAPK + NF-κB pathways via TLR4 activation

**Parameters:** 90 Hz, 0.5 m/s² amplitude, 6 hours acute or daily repeated.

## GENE 6 — KCNJ11 (Insulin Release Gate)

### What KCNJ11 Is

KCNJ11 encodes the Kir6.2 subunit of the ATP-sensitive potassium channel
(K-ATP channel) in pancreatic beta cells. This channel is the molecular
switch for insulin secretion:

When blood glucose rises → glucose enters beta cell → ATP/ADP ratio rises
→ KCNJ11 K-ATP channel closes → beta cell membrane depolarizes → Ca²⁺
influx → insulin vesicle release. KCNJ11 dysfunction = impaired insulin
secretion regardless of glucose level.

The E23K variant (rs5219, T allele) alters channel gating, reducing
insulin secretion efficiency.

### Acoustic Targets for KCNJ11

KCNJ11 is not directly targeted by any frequency — it is a channel
protein whose function depends on the cellular energy state. However:

**Indirect pathway via inflammation:**
TNF-α and IL-1β both impair K-ATP channel function in beta cells via
pro-inflammatory pathways. The 40 Hz anti-inflammatory layer therefore
supports KCNJ11 channel function by removing cytokine-mediated
interference.

**Ca²⁺ metabolism and PIEZO1:**
The Ca²⁺ influx that triggers insulin release (post-depolarization)
operates through a related but separate channel from KCNJ11. Acoustic
PIEZO1 activation creates controlled Ca²⁺ influx in responsive cells.
Beta cells express mechanosensitive channels that respond to physical
vibration — low-amplitude WBV may support Ca²⁺ -mediated insulin
secretion through this pathway.

- **Acoustic support:** 30 Hz WBV + 40 Hz anti-inflammatory (TNF-α/IL-1β ↓)
  → channel dysfunction reduced → insulin secretion efficiency improved

## GENE 7 — APOE (Cholesterol Trafficking)
